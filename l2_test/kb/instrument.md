# DESI Instrument

## Spectrograph arms

Each DESI spectrograph has three arms — **B**, **R**, and **Z** — covering the following wavelength ranges (in Angstroms):

| Arm | Coverage (Å) |
|---|---|
| B | 3600–5800 |
| R | 5760–7620 |
| Z | 7520–9824 |

Note that adjacent arms **overlap**: B and R overlap at 5760–5800 Å, and R and Z overlap at 7520–7620 Å.

## Arm-edge noise

Toward both ends of each arm, the S/N typically degrades and **high-amplitude noise spikes** are common. These spikes are neither genuine emission or absorption features nor sky-line residuals.

Be especially cautious when interpreting features in the two junction regions:

- **5760–5800 Å** (B–R junction)
- **7520–7620 Å** (R–Z junction)

A candidate line falling within these ranges should be treated as suspect and corroborated by other lines before being trusted.
